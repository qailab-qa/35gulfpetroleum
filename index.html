Introduction

NGP & GPM

Northern Gulf Petroleum (NGP)

  • Established in 2004 — Thailand's only independent oil & gas exploration and production company.
  • Oil discoveries in Rossukon field, Block G6/48 in 2009; first oil from Rossukon in November 2023.
  • Cumulative production over 4 million bbls as of January 2026; 2026 forecast sales volume of 5 million bbls.

Gulf Petroleum Myanmar (GPM)

  • Operator of the Yetagun gas field since 2022 with a 79.55% participation interest.
  • Exports natural gas to Thailand via a 204 km pipeline under a 35-year GSA with PTT at ~$10 / MMBtu.
  • Awarded offshore Block M-10 in 2025 — first block since 2013 — to develop six gas discoveries (3–6 Tcf estimated).

Producing Assets Overview

Producing Assets

Thailand

Rossukon Field G6/48

Block G6/48, 259 km (161 miles) northeast of Songkhla, Thailand. Covers 87.74 sq. km with a water depth of 60–65 meters. Operated by Northern Gulf Petroleum.

Concession Contract Term

  • Concession Start Date: 8-Jan-2007
  • Concession End Date: 7-Jan-2036

Key Development

  • First Oil Date: 13-Nov-2023

Rossukon Development

  • Mobile Offshore Production Unit (MOPU)
    • Operated as a normally manned facility
  • Floating Storage and Offloading (FSO)
    • 700,000 bbls capacity
    • Spread mooring system
  • 2× 6″ subsea flexible pipelines for crude and water transfer between MOPU and FSO
  • Crude export via Ship-to-Ship transfer through 10″ export hose

Myanmar

Yetagun in Myanmar

Multi-platform gas development in the Gulf of Martaban, operated by Gulf Petroleum Myanmar with export infrastructure to Thailand.

Yetagun-A (Wellhead Platform)

  • 4 legged with 20 well slots

Yetagun-B (Production Platform)

  • 8 legged with process facilities comprising separation, gas dehydration, gas hydrocarbon dew point control, export gas compression and condensate stabilization
  • 88 Beds living quarters
  • 24″ × 202 km gas export pipeline to POC
  • 6″ × 2.5 km condensate export pipeline to FSO

Yetagun-C (Booster Compressor Platform)

  • 4 legged with process facilities comprising separation, cooling and booster compression

Yetagun-D (Remote Satellite Platform)

  • 4-legged with 4 well slots
  • 4 Beds living quarter and Helideck
  • 10″ × 11.2 km pipeline to Yetagun C
